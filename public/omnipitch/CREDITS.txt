polypitch-1.wasm: OmniPitch's polyphonic pitch detector (Blaine Cowen), built from
polyphonic-pitch-detector at 4c5622a with default features (no vocoder).

It contains:
- Basic Pitch by Spotify (https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch), Apache License 2.0,
  ported to Rust; see LICENSE-basic-pitch. The model weights (nmp.onnx) are Spotify's.
- A version of that model fine-tuned on singing voices (nmp_voices.onnx), trained on the
  Choral Singing Dataset: H. Cuesta, E. Gomez, A. Martorell, F. Loaiciga, "Analysis of
  Intonation in Unison Choir Singing", ICMPC 2018 (https://zenodo.org/record/1286570),
  CC BY 4.0.
- tract (Sonos), MIT/Apache-2.0; rustfft, MIT/Apache-2.0.
