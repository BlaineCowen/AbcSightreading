# Advertising plan: reaching more music teachers

Written 16 November 2026. Goal: more teachers signing up as founding teachers
(free Educator plan through May 2027). Cold email so far: about 7 sign-ups
from about 390 emails.

## The window

Spring sight-reading contests (Texas UIL Concert and Sight-Reading, and the
same kind of contest in other states) mean teachers drill sight reading from
January. **Mid November to February is the best time to reach them.** Pitch
it as contest prep: "practice the exact kind of reading the judges give you".

## In order

### 1. Facebook groups for music teachers (free, start now)

- [ ] Join the big choir director, band director, orchestra and elementary
      music groups, plus Choir Directors of Texas.
- [ ] Read each group's rules on promotion (many allow it only on a set day
      or in a weekly thread).
- [ ] Post as a teacher, not a company: "I teach choir in Houston and built
      this because my choir needed new sight-reading every day. It's free for
      teachers this school year."
- [ ] Attach a 20 to 30 second clip: a student singing and getting graded, or
      a projector drill. (The reels in `marketing/` can be cut down.)
- [ ] Answer every comment the same day.

### 2. Teachers bringing teachers (free, start now)

- [ ] Personal email to each teacher who signed up from outreach: thank you,
      what would you change, and do you know one or two teachers who'd want it.
- [ ] Give them a referral link through the affiliate setup.
- [ ] Build a "Share with a colleague" button on the teacher dashboard.

### 3. TMEA convention, San Antonio, February

- [ ] Decide on a booth now (it costs more and may sell out); Marketing.md in
      Obsidian has the booth costs and dates.
- [ ] Without a booth: go, carry the business cards, talk to teachers.

### 4. Paid ads (once 1 and 2 are running)

- [ ] Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) aimed at music teachers, $10 to $20 a
      day for two weeks. Count sign-ups before spending more.
- [ ] Google Ads on "sight reading generator", "sight singing practice" and
      the like: few searches, so clicks should be cheap, and the people
      searching want exactly this.

### 5. Creators and a part-time helper (see the cost notes below)

- [ ] Music teacher creators with 10k to 100k followers: offer a free Educator
      account plus an affiliate code first; a paid post at that size is
      typically $100 to $1,000. Paid or gifted posts must say #ad (FTC).
- [ ] A music education student, about 8 hours a week at about $20 an hour
      (about $650 a month), to post memes, comment and message creators.
      Give access through Meta Business Suite, not the password.
- [ ] Aim posts at teachers; no contests or content aimed at kids.

## Not yet

Magazine ads and big sponsorships: expensive, and there's no way to tell what
they brought in.
