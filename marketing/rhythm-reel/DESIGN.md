# abcSightReading ad: design

The site's own look, "Recess" (src/styles/globals.css in the app): soft pastel
blocks, big rounded cards, Fredoka headings over Nunito.

## Type

- Display: Fredoka 700 (headlines 76-130px), 600 for kickers.
- Body: Nunito 600-800 (34-40px).
- Wordmark: italic serif "abc" in Action blue, then "SightReading" (or
  "sightreading.com") in Fredoka 700 Ink - as the site's navbar draws it.

## Shapes

Pills for kickers and chips (radius 999px); cards radius 28-40px; soft
shadows (0 24px 60px rgba(21,33,58,0.14)). Footage sits in a white browser
window card on the ground.

## Motion

Playful but tidy: back.out and power3.out entrances, a bouncing ball (the
play-along video's own) as the motif, pastel staggered-block wipes between
scenes. No dashes in copy.

## Don't

No Tailwind default palette, no dark theme, no em dashes, no made-up
statistics or testimonials.
