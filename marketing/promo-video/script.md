# Play-along video promo: script (about 60 s)

Read at an easy pace. Each line's shot is in brackets; the music under each shot is that video's own backing track, ducked under the voice.

| # | Read | Shot |
|---|------|------|
| 1 | Sight reading doesn't have to sound like a metronome in an empty room. | Plain score with a lone click, grey and still; it snaps into the video's colour. |
| 2 | abcSightReading turns any exercise into a play-along video. | Press Video; the bouncing ball starts on bar one, cumbia comes in. |
| 3 | A real groove, a bouncing ball, and the next bar always waiting below. | Ball crossing the top bar, the bottom bar ready; the top turns over. |
| 4 | Clap the rhythm over rock, soul, cumbia or even trap. | Quick cuts: four tracks, a bar each, syllables under the notes. |
| 5 | Or sing it: your melody, in your key, with a band behind you. | Pitched exercise, solfège under the notes, guitar strumming, bass walking. |
| 6 | Slow it down or speed it up | The tempo button stepping 50% to 120%, the groove following without changing key. |
| 7 | Counting, Kodály or your own syllables. Movable do, fixed do or note names. | Label picker flipping through the options on the same bars. |
| 8 | Put it on the board for the whole class, or export it and share it. | Full screen on a TV mock-up, then the Export button and a finished file. |
| 9 | Sight reading music can actually sound like well, music | Finish card with confetti over the last crash. |
| 10 | abcSightReading. Practice real music. | Logo and abcsightreading.com. |

Recording tips: phone voice memo is fine, a quiet room, a short pause between lines (I cut to the pauses). Send the file and I'll build both the 16:9 and 9:16 cuts to your read.
